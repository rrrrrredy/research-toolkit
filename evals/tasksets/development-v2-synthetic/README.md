# Five-family development pack

[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

These original fictional tasks were written on 2026-10-12 for [protocol v2](../../../docs/eval-protocol-v2.md). They have not been run with real models. Their authors have seen the inputs and intended judgments; they are exposed development material, not held-out evidence or factual sources about real organizations.

| Task | Language | Necessary judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wash-water-en` | English | Reconcile litres/cubic metres, totals/intensity and rollout limits |
| `archive-scan-zh` | Chinese | Reconcile different populations and jointly enforce accuracy/throughput |
| `library-reminders-en` | English | Separate group differences, changes and causal identification |
| `backup-budget-zh` | Chinese | Enforce first-year hard constraints and future capacity conditions |
| `ferry-timeline-en` | English | Distinguish superseded targets, conditional plans and current status |

Each folder supplies a reader brief, two complete fictional sources and a coordinator-only design record with provenance, rationale, expected judgments, exclusions and exposure. The five families are different from the October 10 battery, AI risk, banking, memory and productivity tasks; they still share broad research skills. This purposive set is not a representative population sample. Three English and two Chinese tasks are native scenarios, not translations counted twice.

`study.json` contains inspectable placeholders and enables position swaps: at most 10 author requests and 10 judge requests per author model. Preparing it freezes an offline inspection plan; verify and replace provider settings before freezing any real execution plan. No credentials belong here. No outputs, scores or efficacy claims are supplied. The separate [judge controls](../judge-calibration-v2/README.md) do not count as a sixth development family.

For real-world transfer, separately curate rights-cleared sources, independently author tasks after methods freeze, record exposure and rejected candidates, and match retrieval access to the actual question. Do not relabel this public pack as held out.
