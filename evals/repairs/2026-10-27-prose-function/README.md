## Edits and Retentions

The task asks an operations manager to choose this season's delivery option, retaining valid comparisons and changing judgments affected by new orders and prices. The archived review already identified an ambiguous delivery deadline and an insufficiently specific backup-vehicle commitment. This revision changes paragraphs 1 and 6 and the opening sentence of paragraph 5. Paragraphs 2–4 and the rest of paragraph 5 are retained verbatim rather than rewritten to vary their sentence patterns.

| Location | Original wording or function | Treatment and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paragraph 1 | Introduces next week's orders, then says Shuangcheng meets all these requirements | Name the supported capacity, temperature-control and budget conditions. The quote confirms delivery this season; the specific handover date before next week's first delivery remains to be confirmed. Retain the recommendation and comparisons that already hold. |
| Paragraph 6 | Asks to clarify backup dispatch; delivery arrangements appear only at the end | Specify the handover date, a route-specific backup arrival limit and liability as terms still to be agreed. If handover cannot occur in time, delivery arrangements remain unresolved; selecting a qualifying option does not establish fulfillment. |
| Paragraphs 2–4 | Reuses capacity, temperature control and budget to explain each option | Retain. The repeated terms compare different options. The old test does not establish Qingqiao's proposed retrofit performance, and Shuangcheng's price cut is not a performance improvement. Both distinctions advance the decision. |
| Paragraph 5 | Gives independent test conditions; distinguishes customer support from immediate dispatch and a one-hour test margin from a guaranteed rescue window | Retain those conditions, negations and causal explanations. Only replace the opening's broad claim of meeting known requirements with the narrower point that the quote and test results do not establish breakdown coverage. |

## Inspection and Observations

Each paragraph was compared with the original input. The confirmed 76-box demand, 200,000 yuan budget, single-morning delivery and at least ten hours at 2–8°C remain. The 198,000 yuan quote still refers to the original 90-box configuration tested for eleven hours. No delivery promise, reliability ranking or extra fee was invented. Attribution to the independent tests and signed quote, the unverified Qingqiao retrofit, Yuanquan's insufficient eight hours, and the effect of waiting and transfer on the temperature-control margin remain. The task supplies its material and requests prose only; the original has no external citations. None were added or removed, and links to the material remain above.

The observable changes are a more precise opening conclusion and final actions tied to the unresolved date and route-specific arrival limit, with existing comparisons and mechanisms retained. No whole paragraph needed removal for style, and the conclusion was not shortened by dropping qualifications.

This shows that these local edits can be made from the existing material. It does not establish that the revised guidance will make an Agent choose similarly on new tasks. Reader comprehension, user rework and time savings were not measured. No first draft was regenerated or extra review added, and this revision is not counted in the original comparison.
