# Research brief

## question

Private-credit funds piloting on-chain settlement: what operational and disclosure risks matter for LP reporting?

## audience

Private-credit GP ops/compliance and LP due-diligence readers.

## scope

Synthetic source pack only. Cover settlement ops, oracle/custody, valuation timing, and LP disclosure. Finance/tech long memo. No live web.

## output

final.md, about 1500–2500 whitespace words: risk map, evidence bounds, counter-evidence, disclosure recommendations. Registries and checklist separate.

## depth

Lite long-form finance/tech memo; not a crypto product pitch.

## evidence_standard

Synthetic sources only; do not treat pilot success as production readiness; disclose synthetic materials and no independent review.

## outline

1. Judgment for LP reporting
2. What the pilot is / is not
3. Operational and disclosure risk map
4. Metrics: clean vs exception paths
5. Counter-claims to reject
6. Disclosure recommendations and limits
