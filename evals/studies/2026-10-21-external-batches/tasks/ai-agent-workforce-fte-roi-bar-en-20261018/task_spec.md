# Research brief

## question

For enterprise buyers evaluating “AI agent workforce” / digital-employee vendors, does evidence of demo or pilot task completion support production claims of FTE replacement / headcount ROI through 2027—or must buyers treat headcount-replacement claims as a false proposition without workflow ownership, exception handling, and auditable override trails?

## audience

Enterprise IT/ops buyers and CFO / workforce-planning sponsors.

## scope

Synthetic source pack only; evidence tiers (demo vs production ownership); ROI claim mapping; counter-evidence (hidden human exception queues); kill criteria for FTE-replacement marketing. No live web.

## output

final.md, ≥1500 whitespace words (suggested 1500–2500): tiers, ROI mapping, counter-evidence, kill criteria. Registries and checklist separate.

## depth

Lite long-form procurement/ROI memo; not an agent-tool bake-off.

## evidence_standard

Synthetic sources disclosed; do not treat demo completion as FTE replacement; disclose no independent review.
