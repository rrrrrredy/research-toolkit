## Evidence appendix: material distinctions and remaining gaps

**Public availability and record versions.** The registry snapshots used for the central clinical histories were last publicly updated before the cutoff: rentosertib phase IIa,11December2025; oral phase III,13October2026; EXS21546 healthy-volunteer study,16May2022; IGNITE,8November2023; SYCAMORE,22October2026; TUPELO,5June2026. A trial's administrative update date is not the date of its clinical outcome. The SYCAMORE results posted in October2026 are available for this report, although the decision to stop development was announced in May2025.

**Rentosertib dates and analyses.** The phase IIa publication describes trial conduct from19July2023 through11June2024; its registry records actual study start19June2023 and completion8August2024. Those fields were not reconciled by inventing a common date. The report uses the publication for its12-week analysis and the registry for the specifically labeled registry milestones. Twenty-two missing week-12 spirometry measurements differ from16 overall trial discontinuations. The observed-case comparison uses10 high-dose and14 placebo patients; ANCOVA with multiple imputation uses randomized groups of18 and17. Their descriptive differences are118.7mL and97.4mL respectively, with no new between-group confidence interval inferred. The available Reporting Summary was examined; the full protocol and statistical analysis plan were not obtained. A separate outlier-exclusion sensitivity analysis mentioned in that summary is not substituted for the main analysis.

**Discontinuation language.** DSP-1181's disclosed unmet phase I criterion remains unspecified. EXS21546's portfolio-reprioritization narrative is supplemented by its registry's therapeutic-index explanation. REC-994's final registry status of completed records study closure and does not imply that development continued. The source record supports these distinctions without assigning an undisclosed toxicity, economic motive or patient-level causal explanation.

**REC-4881 populations and versions.** The2November2026 conference presentation and3Q26 earnings presentation are different documents. The report uses the disclosed September2026 data cutoff and distinguishes26 safety-evaluable participants,21 phase II participants at4mg,16 efficacy-evaluable participants,15 week-13 assessments and13 week-25 assessments. Earlier reports with smaller cohorts are not pooled. The efficacy definition's exposure requirement and missing endoscopies constrain an all-treated interpretation. Natural-history data are contextual rather than a concurrent control.

**Aggregate AI estimates.** The historical2024 analysis is discussed using its published completed-program numerators and denominators, broad inclusion rules and COVID-19 exclusion. This report does not re-audit every row of that study's supplementary cohort or update it to2026. The calculated Wilson intervals describe sampling uncertainty only. They do not establish an AI-versus-conventional treatment effect on development success.

**Commercial disclosure.** Program licenses, platform technology ownership and data reuse are separate rights. Redacted Sanofi contract allocations and undisclosed candidate names or targets are left unresolved. No royalty relationship with BenevolentAI is inferred from its baricitinib contribution. No unrelated Insilico partnership payment is assigned to rentosertib. Future milestone amounts and trial plans remain contingent.

**Access and author calculations.** Original HTML, PDFs, registry JSON, retrieval metadata and author reading ranges are retained with the research materials. Some issuer PDFs and supplementary downloads failed; web-extracted primary text was used where available, with the failure and actual reading method recorded. No model trial or clinical experiment was performed for this report. The calculations are explicit arithmetic on reported counts and means, a descriptive interval calculation, and contract-ceiling arithmetic. Independent review of this manuscript is a separate step from author production.
