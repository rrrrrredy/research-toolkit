DeepSeek's reported training estimate and the broader cost-model literature support making that boundary explicit; they do not establish a universal recovery charge per token. Output and human intervention may dominate short-task economics even when input tokens are cheap. A mechanical calibration using GPT-4.1 mini's April 14, 2025 launch prices gives (1,000×0.40 + 200×1.60)/1,000,000 = USD 0.00072 for one assumed request. Separately, an assumed 10% review rate, two minutes per review, and USD 30/hour adds USD 0.10 per incoming task. These are arithmetic illustrations, not measured support costs or current-price claims. [TE03] ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4-1/))\nC.
