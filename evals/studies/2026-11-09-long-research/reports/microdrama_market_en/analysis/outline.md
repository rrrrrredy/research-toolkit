Reconcile source series by definition and period, without averaging them. Establish this unit before relying on any growth premise.
2. **Demand and monetization.** Separate audience reach, time, frequency, payment conversion, spend, and ad yield. Compare 2024/2025 survey reach with 2026 app migration. 3. **Who controls distribution?** Compare Douyin/Hongguo, Kuaishou, WeChat/mini-program operators, and long-video/native players where the actual evidence supports inclusion. Cover recommendation/search, customer relationship, IP, exclusivity, multi-channel operations, commissions and settlement, and relevant mixed models. Avoid causal rankings from unmatched audiences.
4. **Who earns and who finances?** Trace user and advertiser cash through media sellers, platform settlement, distributors, rights owners, and producers. Use disclosed company cases, explicitly retaining consolidation and domestic/overseas boundaries. Execute a labeled scenario with paid conversion/acquisition, production cost, refunds, sharing, viewing/completion and retention sensitivities. Distinguish cash and recognized revenue, and gross margin from contribution and net profit.
5. **Rules that change the investment.** Explain the dated content classification regime, commissioning/filing obligations, platform compliance, minors/refunds, and copyright as operating and financing variables. Explain Chinese concepts naturally. Keep announcements separate from realized outcomes.
6. **Decisions.** Integrate the units into conditions for platform investment, production/distribution contracts, and investor diligence, including contrary evidence and observations that would reverse the judgment.
