# Long-research benchmark: November 2026 baseline

[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

This study uses the current Research Toolkit research path on **13 independent questions and 15 report assignments**. Seven primary reports are Chinese and six are English. Two further reports independently answer the microdrama question in English and the test-time-compute question in Chinese, using their respective frozen materials without reading the other-language answer.

The number follows the research coverage: eleven user-selected directions, plus the two new homepage subjects. It is not a fixed benchmark quota. A revision, another language, another model or another run does not create another independent question.

The full requirements are in [cases](../../cases/). They ask for developed research, rather than a fixed section count or a short answer to one isolated point. An executive summary can accompany the complete report.

## Execute another run

1. Select the question files and a Toolkit revision. Keep that selection, its original prompts and the model/runtime configuration with the run. The existing runner accepts repeated `--case` selections; omitting them retains the all-cases behavior.
2. Follow each case's material mode. Primary cases investigate public material through their declared cutoff. Frozen-language assignments use the captured originals for that question and receive no other-language report. Retain actual access failures, publication/version dates, original files and hashes. For a later cutoff or a changed material set, create a separately identified run.
3. Use the existing research entry to plan, investigate, develop substantive units and assemble the complete report. Preserve the first complete manuscript, author checks, unit outputs and necessary revisions. Agent tools or API calls may supply the independent contexts.
4. Complete the existing seven content perspectives, applicable domain review, independent audit and separate final acceptance. Initial reviewers receive the original question, complete current manuscript and necessary originals, without other initial judgments or an expected result. Changed work returns to the responsible perspective with its actual earlier input and assessment.
5. Keep first-submission findings separate from accepted revisions. Record the paragraph, source or requirement that supports each necessary correction. Use the existing [five research-quality dimensions](../../../docs/report-evaluation-standard.md) when interpreting results; a total score is optional and must not replace located findings.
6. Run the existing mechanical evaluator against the actual completed task. These cases use `reports/current.md` as the registered delivery artifact and an identical `final.md` export for the evaluator. Retain the real completion result and original failures, rather than creating placeholder state.

Execution measurements describe what occurred. Report quality and stopping decisions follow the research requirements, evidence and current complete output. Comparing Toolkit with another version or a native agent is a separate study design; this baseline establishes tasks and observable results for subsequent use.

### What changed in the manuscripts

- **Microdrama:** the Chinese report now separates different observation periods and statistical populations when explaining growth, and correctly records the PKU estimate's exclusion of overseas revenue. The independently written English report retains its own argument and corrected source-unit notes.
- **Test-time compute:** the English report defines overthinking and accuracy-drop units and connects execution back to the decision tree's completion check. Publication inspection also found internal citation labels missed by the original acceptance; the author replaced their display with paper names while retaining URLs, versions and substantive text. The earlier delivered version and missed finding remain in `report-history/`. The Chinese worked example explains expected spending versus a hard per-task cap and exposes its second-prefill assumption. The saved calculations were reused.
- **Model benchmarks:** the audit found omissions after content review: τ-bench's action/output reward and its confirmation-policy blind spot, plus original generation, execution and cost conditions. The relevant passages were revised and the original adverse audit remains available.
- **Personal agents:** generic architecture observations were developed into domestic/international comparisons of travel and expense tasks, including authority, supported actions, state and resulting records.
- **EV batteries:** a requirements review identified missing evidence for mature product deployment and an undeveloped volume comparison. The revision adds named delivery/application evidence and an explicit hypothetical pack-volume calculation, connected to vehicle selection.
- **Instant retail and AI drug discovery:** the warehouse report makes its original acquisition and ramp-up assumptions visible in the prose; the drug-discovery report corrects compounds versus iteration cycles. Other changes remove production narration while preserving dates, version conditions and relevant scientific or operating limits.

- **ByteDance advertising:** [retained revision](reports/bytedance_advertising_zh/analysis/change-note-full-02.md) replaces the Smart+ search/opening narrative with its October 2026 document version and account rollout condition.
- **Embodied deployment:** [retained revision](reports/embodied_deployment_zh/analysis/change-note-full-01.md) removes the author identity and pending-review statement while retaining evidence and calculation references.
- **On-device models:** [retained revision](reports/on_device_models_en/analysis/change-note-full-01.md) removes author/review and acquisition-handoff narration, retaining the Android performance-lock conditions and tables.
- **Post-training talent:** [retained revision](reports/post_training_talent_zh/analysis/change-note-full-01.md) removes the opening pending-review statement; dates, public-source method, people and affiliations remain.
- **Token economics:** [retained revision](reports/token_economics_en/analysis/change-note-full-01.md) restores appendix spacing and removes author/review metadata without changing the commercial conditions and scenario qualifications.
- **Verifiable self-improvement:** [retained revision](reports/verifiable_self_improvement_en/analysis/change-note-full-02.md) replaces source-reading narration with the actual dataset split and version conditions used in the analysis.

These entries cover all 15 assignments, including editorial changes as well as substantive corrections. No aggregate numeric quality scoring was performed; the available judgments are the original independent findings and their dispositions.

These are located improvements to the retained manuscripts. The run is a development baseline, not a fixed-model before/after study: it began on the v2.3.4-based working tree, continued through the fixes recorded here, and switched from Astra to authorized native Codex execution after the retained explicit credit-exhaustion response. The original model/run identity remains with each output. No overall causal quality or rework-reduction estimate is assigned.

### Execution and engineering evidence

The existing mechanical evaluator's output is saved separately from the independent content judgments. [Verification records](verification/) include the necessary CLI scenarios, targeted source-ID mapping, binary-original restoration and CSV normalization, mandatory repository checks, and their original failures and specific corrections. Unchanged passing checks were reused. An unchanged assignment already imported into the task was reused rather than counted as another review.

The [office-report editorial revision](office-editorial-revision/README.md) is a separate revision of the historical November 5 report, with its original and independent findings retained. It is not a sixteenth report assignment or another question in this cohort.

The first mechanical aggregation passed 13 reports and falsely flagged two: repeated Markdown table separators in the Chinese microdrama report, and a question mark inside a repeatedly cited paper title in the English compute report. The parsing repair preserved the reports. Only those two failed cases were rerun and passed; three existing repetition controls still detect actual repeated prose. The original aggregate and targeted results are retained in `verification/final-artifact-checks/`.
