L27: Extended access is a service that enables you to use the library when staff are not present.
L28: 
L29: Extended access is available at:
L30: 
L31:   * The Campus Library
L32:   * Clevedon Library
L33:   * Nailsea Library
L34:   * Portishead Library
L35:   * Winscombe Library
L36:   * Worle Library and Children’s Centre
L37:   * Yatton Library and Children’s Centre
L38: ### Benefits of extended access
L39: 
L40: If you are a registered library customer and have attended a briefing, you can access the above libraries outside of staffed hours, including evenings (5.15 to 8pm), weekends (8am to 8pm) and most bank holidays.
L41: 
L42: You will be able to:
L43:   * scan your library card and use your pin to enter the library
L44:   * connect to our free Wi-Fi
L45:   * use a computer
L46:   * print and photocopy
L47:   * access online e-resources
L48:   * use the self service kiosks to issue, return, and renew items and pay charges
L49:   * collect reserved books and items
L50:   * use the library to meet others, read and study
L51:   * read books and newspapers
L52: 
L53: For safety reasons there are no toilet facilities available during extended access hours.
L54: ## Booking a briefing
L55: 
L56: Before you can use extended access, you need to attend a briefing.
L57: 
L58: Sessions are free and take 20 to 30 minutes.
L59: 
L60: If you are not currently a library member, you can sign up at your induction. Just bring a current ID which shows your name and address.
L61: 
L62: You can sign up for your briefing session via Eventbrite below. If there are no suitable induction dates for you, please contact the library directly to book an alternative time.

L65: 
L66: Using extended access
L67: 
L68: To use extended access you must
L69: 
L70:   * have a current library membership with us or another LibrariesWest authority (Bath and North East Somerset, Dorset, Bristol, Poole, Somerset and South Gloucestershire)
L71:   * be at least 16 years old, or 15 if you have parental permission. If you are aged 15 or under you can use extended access if you are accompanied by a parent/carer who has registered to use the service
L72: You access the library by scanning your library card on the card reader outside the main library doors and entering your PIN. The doors will automatically open for you when you wish to exit the library.
L73: 
L74: When the extended access time is coming to an end, you will hear a pre-recorded message. If you are using one of our public computers, they will automatically log off 10 to 15 minutes before the library closes.
L75: If an emergency occurs while you are in an unstaffed library, call our out-of-hours number on the help phone.
L76: 
L77: If the emergency is very serious you can use the same help phone to call 999.
L78: 
L79: You will be shown the help phone at your briefing.
L80: 
L81: You can also use the help phone at certain times to call library staff if something isn’t working correctly.
L82: If you don’t need urgent assistance, you can let our library staff know by leaving a note in the comments box for them to pick up when they are next in, or email them.
L83: ##
L84: 
L85: Accessibility
L86: 
L87: If you are currently able to use the library, you will be able to use it during extended access hours, as long as you have had an induction.
L88: 
L89: There will be no staff on site during extended access, so there will be nobody to assist you should you need it. If you need a carer with you, they will also have to be a registered library user and will need to have attended a briefing.

L91: 
L92: Safety information
L93: 
L94: You should familiarise yourself with the safety information for each library where you intend to use extended access.
L95: ### Clevedon Library
L96:   * there are three emergency exits - the front entrance, the door through the Waverley room to the car park, and the door from the children’s library area through the staff lobby to the car park (important: this exit is only 79cm wide)
L97:   * the emergency exit from the children’s library area is only 79cm wide and may not be accessible for people using wheelchairs or mobility scooters. Please speak to a member of staff at your briefing to make alternative arrangements
L98:   * in the event of a fire alarm or a power failure, you’ll be able to push the exit doors open - the front door will be held open automatically
L99:   * the help phone and a list of contact numbers is in the entrance foyer near the front door
L100:   * toilets and the Balmoral room are not available during extended access hours
L101:   * the Waverley room may be used by partners or hirers separately during extended access, using the external entrance only
L102: ### Nailsea Library
L103: 
L104:   * the emergency exit is the main entrance
L105:   * in the event of a fire alarm or power failure, the door is automatically held open
L106:   * the help phone is on the lower staff desk
L107: ### Portishead Library
L108:   * there are two emergency exits - the main entrance and the fire doors at the back of the library
L109:   * in the event of a fire alarm or power failure, front doors will be held open automatically. If the alarm is not sounding, you’ll need to push the bar on the fire doors at the back of the library to release them
L110:   * the help phone is located next to the photocopier
L111:   * the library meeting room is not available during extended access. It may be used by external groups with advance bookings who will have their own access arrangements
L112: ### Yatton Library
L113:   * there are three emergency exits - the front entrance, the back door to the car park, and the door leading to the Yatton Moor room
L114:   * in the event of a fire alarm or a power failure, you’ll be able to push the doors open - the front door will be held open automatically
L115:   * the help phone and a list of contact numbers is in the main library space, to the right of the main doors
L116:   * the upstairs area, lift and toilets are not available during extended access hours
L117:   * the upstairs and the lift may be used by groups with advance bookings who will have their own access arrangements
L118:   * the Greta Lewis room and the Yatton Moor room may be used by partners or external hirers separately during extended access
L119: ### Worle Library
L120: 
L121:   * there are two emergency exits - the front entrance and the fire door in the children’s area opposite the toilets
L122:   * in the event of a fire alarm or power failure, you’ll be able to push the doors open - the front doors will be held open automatically
L123:   * the help phone is on the desk by the computers, next to the DVDs
L124:   * the children’s centre areas may be used by staff or groups separately during extended access
L125: ### Winscombe Library
L126: 
L127:   * the emergency exit is the main entrance
L128:   * in the event of a fire alarm or power failure, doors are automatically held open
L129:   * the help phone is on the desk between the two public computers
