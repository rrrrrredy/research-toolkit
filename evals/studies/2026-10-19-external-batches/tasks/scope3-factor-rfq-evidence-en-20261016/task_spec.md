# Research brief

## question

How should mid-market manufacturers set an evidence bar for third-party Scope-3 emissions factors before using them in customer RFQ sustainability questionnaires through 2027?

## audience

Manufacturing sustainability lead and commercial RFQ owners.

## scope

Synthetic source pack only; cover evidence tiers vs vendor claims, failure modes, counter-evidence, disclosure stance and kill criteria through 2027. No live web.

## output

final.md, ≥1500 whitespace words (suggested 1500–2500): evidence bar, failure modes, counter-evidence, disclosure/kill criteria. Registries and checklist separate.

## depth

Lite long-form controls/disclosure memo.

## evidence_standard

Synthetic sources disclosed; do not promote vendor factor packs as audit-ready; disclose no independent review.
