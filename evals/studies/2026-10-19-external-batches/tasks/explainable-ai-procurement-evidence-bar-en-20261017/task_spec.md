# Research brief

## question

For enterprise buyers and model-risk reviewers evaluating vendor “explainable AI” claims for high-stakes workflows (including adverse-action or customer-facing decision support), does post-hoc natural-language explanation evidence suffice versus requiring pre-deployment audit trails of features/rules/logs—and can that bar support production procurement and questionnaire answers through 2027?

## audience

Enterprise AI procurement, model risk, and vendor security/compliance.

## scope

Synthetic source pack only; evidence tiers (post-hoc NL vs pre-hoc auditability), questionnaire mapping, counter-evidence, kill criteria through 2027. No live web.

## output

final.md, ≥1500 whitespace words (suggested 1500–2500): evidence tiers, questionnaire mapping, counter-evidence, kill criteria. Registries and checklist separate.

## depth

Lite long-form procurement/model-risk memo on explainability evidence bars.

## evidence_standard

Synthetic sources disclosed; do not equate post-hoc NL explanations with auditable decision paths; disclose no independent review.
