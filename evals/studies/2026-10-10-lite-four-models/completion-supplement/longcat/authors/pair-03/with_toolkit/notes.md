### 5. Section Drafting Notes

- **§1 Conclusion**: Three-pronged rejection — no AI causality, mfg weakness, revision instability. Sets up the rest.
- **§2 Measurement bases**: Defines three口径 with concrete numbers; shows how mixing them distorts (nonfarm Q4 1.5% vs annual 2.7%; mfg all three weak).
- **§3 Sector comparison**: Nonfarm strong but not transferable (C7). Mfg weak with internal split: durable declining, nondurable the only bright spot. Mfg productivity gains driven by hours falling faster than output — contraction signature, not efficiency boom. ULC rising = margin pressure.
- **§4 Revision impact**: Specific magnitudes show mfg was overstated in preliminary data; ULC understated. Nonfarm Q3 ULC 2pp swing demonstrates instability. Implication: use revised data but plan around ranges.
- **§5 Positive/negative**: Steel-mans both sides. Positive: nonfarm acceleration, real comp rising, nondurable. Negative: mfg output/hours both down, ULC up, durable down, cycle cumulative far below long-term.
- **§6 Recommendations**: Conditional — no immediate raise; targeted raise only if firm-level evidence confirms conversion; lists specific evidence to collect (not fabricate); notes Q1 2025 release date as next data point.
