**No, the new ferry cannot be advertised as available from 1 March.** That date is a withdrawn plan, not a current commitment.

The operator originally proposed launching passenger service on 1 March, but on 10 February it withdrew that target after the vessel delivery slipped and proposed 15 April as the earliest possible launch instead, still subject to delivery, trials and final operating permission (S1). This makes 1 March a superseded plan and 15 April a proposed, conditional date only. No tickets had been released as of 10 February (S1), and there was still no booking release, confirmed timetable, or final passenger-service permission as of 14 February (S2).

What the port log *does* permit is limited: a 12 February entry authorizes non-passenger trial sailings during March (S2). Trial permission is not passenger authorization — it explicitly does not permit carrying paying passengers (S2), and the vessel had not yet been delivered (S2).

**Before accepting or advertising any bookings, verify:**
1. Vessel delivery has occurred.
2. Trials have been completed successfully.
3. Final operating permission for passenger service is in place.
4. A confirmed timetable and ticket release are announced by the operator.

Until all four are confirmed, the desk should describe the service as "planned, date not confirmed" and decline to promise either the 1 March or 15 April date.
