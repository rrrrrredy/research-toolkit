# Ferry Availability Status — Visitor Desk Note

**As of 15 February 2026**

The new ferry **cannot** be advertised as available from 1 March. The operator’s 1 February proposal of a 1 March launch was contingent on vessel delivery and operating permission (S1), but on 10 February the operator withdrew that target after delivery slipped, proposing 15 April as the earliest possible passenger launch, still subject to delivery, trials and final operating permission (S1). The 1 March date is therefore superseded and should not appear in any booking material.

The 15 April proposal is likewise not a current commitment: it is an earliest-possible date, conditional on outstanding items (S1). The port log allows only non-passenger trial sailings during March and expressly does not authorize carrying paying passengers (S2). No final passenger-service permission, confirmed timetable or booking release exists, and the vessel had not been delivered by 14 February (S2).

Before accepting bookings, the desk must verify: vessel delivery; completion of trials; final passenger-service operating permission; a confirmed timetable; and formal booking release (S1, S2). No tickets had been released as of 10 February (S1). Any advertisement should describe the service as proposed, not available.
