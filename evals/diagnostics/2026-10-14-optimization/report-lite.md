# Trial Maple within the current subscription ceiling

Maple is the eligible trial choice for the six-person support team. Its $28 monthly plan covers all six active users and permits manual CSV exports of all tickets. That leaves $7 below the $35 subscription ceiling. Cedar would cost six times $6, or $36 per month, exceeding the ceiling by $1. Scheduled exports and single sign-on do not make that current quote affordable. These are comparisons of fictional plan notes supplied for this exercise, not verified market offers. [Helpdesk plan notes](https://github.com/rrrrrredy/research-toolkit/blob/main/examples/lite/sources.md)

## What the choice buys

Weekly export does not require scheduling in this brief: the team can trigger the export manually. Maple therefore meets the stated seat, subscription and export-method requirements. Cedar's scheduling would remove the need to initiate each export, and its single sign-on could be useful, but neither is required now. There is no evidence of measured reliability, support quality or onboarding speed for either plan, so those cannot justify a preference. The subscription comparison also excludes staff time and migration cost; the $7 headroom is not an estimate of total savings.

The main uncertainty is whether the exported CSV includes the fields the team needs. Neither note lists columns, and the team has not yet supplied its required field list. This does not prevent selecting a trial, but it prevents concluding that Maple is ready for operational adoption. Both plans are billed monthly without a minimum term; a trial recommendation is not authorization to buy a subscription.

## Start the trial and define what would change the decision

The support lead should first list the required ticket fields with the people who use the weekly export. If an authorized trial is available, a designated support operator should create representative test tickets, export them manually, and compare every required field and ticket against the list. Include examples that reflect the team's own workflow instead of assuming particular columns or export behavior. Record missing values, unusable formatting and any failed export; these are proposed checks, not observed results.

The lead should also assign an owner and backup for the weekly export. Before adopting Maple, confirm that the actual subscription terms still cover six seats within $35 and that the team accepts manual scheduling. If required fields are absent, keep adoption pending and seek a suitable plan. If automated exports or single sign-on become mandatory, Maple no longer meets the new requirements. Cedar could then be reconsidered only with an affordable confirmed quote or an explicitly changed budget, followed by the same export checks. Neither future discounts nor untested capabilities count as present eligibility.
