# Historical checklist-task compatibility

This page explains saved `profile=lite` records. It is not a current research entry or a route to author-only delivery. Completed historical artifacts and receipts retain their original meaning; continue research through the [shared methods](../SKILL.md), reuse the existing materials and complete current independent review, audit and acceptance.

## final — Historical author checklist

Older tasks stored `state/final_checklist.json` with six author-assessed items:

| Key | Meaning of the historical author check |
| --- | --- |
| `requirements` | Requested questions, follow-ups, format and explicit length requirements |
| `claims_and_sources` | Important claims and citations against sources actually read |
| `counterevidence` | Counterevidence and uncertainty at the affected conclusions |
| `sections` | Necessary explanations and coverage without repeated filler |
| `reader_edit` | Reader usefulness, repeated/off-topic material and production narration |
| `limitations` | Material evidence limits and the lack of independent review |

The saved `passed` and `evidence` fields describe what that author recorded. They must not be relabelled as independent review, audit or final acceptance. Preserve failures, generic or mistaken pass claims and subsequent corrections as separate historical evidence.

Depending on the recorded schema, a receipt binds the report and delivery message, or additionally the task specification, source/claim registries, requirement ledger, declared local reading files and optional `evidence_files`. Read the actual receipt version rather than inventing stronger historical bindings. Text hashes use LF-normalized bytes. A remote URL in a source record does not mean it was downloaded or read.

Existing `length_constraint` fields retain their recorded unit and scope: `whitespace_words` or `nonwhitespace_characters`, `whole_artifact`, and inclusive `min` and/or `max`. They measure the explicitly agreed scope, not research depth. The [counting reference](../docs/delivery-verification.md#explicit-length-requirements) explains those fields.

Do not resubmit an old checklist as a substitute for the current completion requirements. Preserve the original artifact, read the effective requirements and full research material, and continue with the [historical task compatibility path](../docs/usage-modes.md#historical-task-compatibility).
