# Helpdesk plans: a fictional source pack

[English](sources.md) · [简体中文](sources.zh-CN.md)

These two repository-authored notes describe invented services. Prices and capabilities are fictional, for a short Lite exercise only; they are not market evidence or evaluation results. Use only these supplied materials.

## S1 — Cedar plan note

Cedar costs $6 per active user per month, billed monthly, with no minimum term. Every team member needs an active seat. The plan includes manual CSV exports of all tickets, scheduled weekly CSV exports, and single sign-on. The note does not list exported CSV columns. It gives no measured onboarding time, reliability or support-response data.

## S2 — Maple plan note

Maple costs $28 per month for up to six active users, billed monthly, with no minimum term. The plan includes manual CSV exports of all tickets. Scheduled exports and single sign-on are not included. The note does not list exported CSV columns. It gives no measured onboarding time, reliability or support-response data.

## Decision context

Six support staff each need a seat. Their subscription ceiling is $35 per month. They need one CSV export each week and can trigger it manually. Single sign-on is optional. This is a choice of which service to trial, not authorization to purchase. The trial must check that CSV exports contain the fields the team needs; the required field list has not yet been supplied. Staff time and migration cost are outside the subscription ceiling and have not been measured.
