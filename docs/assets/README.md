# Showcase assets

The English homepage and README use the original English **APIs or browsers for AI agents** case from 10 October 2026. The Chinese homepage and README retain the September 2026 office-agents case and its existing assets.

- api-browser-case.en*.png: native English report excerpts for the README, with desktop/mobile and light/dark variants.
- api-browser-preview.en.png: the English homepage excerpt used as the video poster.
- api-browser-social.en.png: the English Open Graph preview.
- api-browser-walkthrough.en.mp4 and .gif: recordings of the native English case browser, report and research-request entry. They demonstrate the published interface, not an agent conducting a study.
- native-case.en.json: the original English excerpts, actual migration-cost correction, reasoning and review history.
- case-study.json: the unchanged historical bilingual office-agents comparison. Its English text consists of labeled translations of Chinese passages.
- repository-case.*.png, readme-preview.*.png, research-method.*.png, case-walkthrough.mp4, case-walkthrough.gif, their Chinese variants, and social-preview.png: retained historical office-agents assets. Chinese surfaces continue to use their Chinese variants.
- reading-preferences.js: language, color theme and topic context across the case browser and document readers.

The English case is original research from primary English documentation. Its support-ticket workflow is hypothetical and its cost model unmeasured. The historical office-agents case is public-source research without installed-product testing. Neither draft-to-report record is a controlled with/without-toolkit experiment.
