# Historical paired-writing study records

[English](eval-protocol-v2.md) | [简体中文](eval-protocol-v2.zh-CN.md)

Current research and benchmarking use the [shared entry](../SKILL.md) and [evals execution arrangements](../evals/README.md). The old single-response research executor and its prepare/run commands have been removed.

Original October 2026 briefs, method snapshots, requests, failures and judgments remain in [studies](../evals/studies/). They describe the work actually performed at that time, not method choices for current tasks. Original files and hashes remain unchanged; use the recorded historical release when reproducing its execution code.

The repository retains the functions needed to read original records, parse outputs and verify historical statistics. Ordinary research and repairs do not execute the retired experiment or derive their responsibilities from its parameters.
