## Direct prose

The opening of the workspace section moves from a broad value claim to how the products organize work.

**Replace rhetorical setup with actions and comparison criteria.**

The final report shortens the rhetorical setup and a vague closing, then names tools, context, and device availability.

### Changes

**Initial draft · Translated excerpt**

> The value of independent office agents lies not in moving a chat box onto the desktop, but in bringing materials, execution, and revisions into one stretch of work. For people who frequently organize files from different sources, this approach can reduce uploading, downloading, and switching tools. Yet keeping files on a computer does not mean tasks run continuously or all processing happens offline. The differences between Kimi Work and Coze show that independent workspaces do not share a single implementation.

The opening frames value through a rhetorical contrast; the closing only says implementations differ.

[Original passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/original.zh-CN.html#L47)

**Final report · Translated excerpt**

> Independent office agents organize materials, execution, and revisions in one workspace. For people who frequently organize files from different sources, this approach can reduce uploading, downloading, and switching tools. Continuous operation and the location of data processing still depend on the implementation. The differences between Kimi Work and Coze center on who supplies tools, who retains context, and who keeps the device online.

The report states the mechanism directly and identifies comparison criteria, while retaining its conditions.

[Original passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/revised.zh-CN.html#L55)

**What changed**

- **Shortened**：Remove the rhetorical setup and state how materials, execution, and revisions share a workspace.
- **Made specific**：Turn “implementations differ” into tools, context, and device availability. Keep the conditional benefit of less file handling.

### Reasoning

**Which wording adds little**

Moving a chat box is not the mechanism examined in this paragraph. Using it as a contrast delays the point. Saying implementations differ gives the reader no criteria.

**Why this revision helps**

The final paragraph states the workspace mechanism and identifies three conditions. Readers can compare the products on tools, context, and device availability.

**Decision and tradeoffs**

- **Chosen**：Express the judgment through actions, objects, and conditions, with less rhetorical setup.
- **Retained**：Less file handling is a possible benefit; continuous operation and processing location still depend on the implementation.
- **Not inferred**：This edit neither bans contrastive sentences nor identifies whether a passage was written by AI.

[Initial passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/original.zh-CN.html#L47) · [Final passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/revised.zh-CN.html#L55) · [Author editing record (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/reviews.html#writing-record)

### Review

- **Initial and later drafts**：The paragraph opened with a value contrast and ended by saying implementations differ. Early retained drafts kept that wording.
- **Author editing check**：The author found that repeated limitations and verification language displaced substantive product analysis.
- **Rewritten intermediate draft**：The next retained draft stated the workspace mechanism and named tools, context, and device availability.
- **Final report**：The final report retained that revision, including the conditional benefit and operating constraints.

The passage change is verifiable in the manuscripts. Its editing context is an author check, not a model’s sentence-by-sentence instruction.

## aily and Doubao Work

Feishu introduced aily custom agents in April; its late-August article used the name Doubao Work. This comparison concerns the relationship between those products.

**The retained sources do not establish that Doubao Work inherits from aily.**

Two product descriptions can be analyzed on their own. Their dates and similar direction do not establish a version lineage.

### Changes

**Initial draft · Translated excerpt**

> This is evidence of continuity between the current product description and the earlier feature direction

The same paragraph already cautioned against assuming a one-to-one transfer of features and names.

[Original passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/original.zh-CN.html#L77)

**Final report · Translated excerpt**

> The two points in time show product directions in object operations and organizational-context collaboration; they do not establish version inheritance between aily and Doubao Work

The migration caveat remains; the report now explicitly limits what the two sources establish.

[Original passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/revised.zh-CN.html#L89)

**What changed**

- **Narrowed**：“Evidence of continuity” becomes product directions at two points in time, without a claim of version inheritance.
- **Retained**：Both official product descriptions and the warning against carrying earlier capabilities over to the later product.

### Reasoning

**What each source supports**

The April source describes aily operating on documents, multidimensional tables, and tasks. The late-August article describes Doubao Work using organizational context and returning results to collaboration.

**Why the inference was narrowed**

Similar functions and successive dates do not establish renaming, version inheritance, or feature migration. The author’s follow-up did not obtain that evidence, so wording implying continuity was removed.

**Decision and tradeoffs**

- **Chosen**：Analyze each source without joining them into a verified product lineage.
- **Tradeoff**：The product relationship stays unresolved. Comparisons must use capabilities supported by each source separately, without merging their feature lists.
- **What could change it**：An explicit renaming notice, version mapping, or feature-migration document would support revisiting the relationship.

[Feishu aily source](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/sources.html#S01) · [Doubao Work source](https://www.feishu.cn/content/article/7680041109057178898)

### Review

- **Initial and later drafts**：“Evidence of continuity” remained alongside the migration caveat. The wording implied a stronger relationship than the available sources established.
- **Review finding**：A model review flagged that “evidence of continuity” could imply version inheritance. The author accepted a precision correction.
- **Source check and decision**：The follow-up obtained no renaming or migration proof. An aily domain in site navigation was not treated as proof either. Both dated product descriptions were kept.
- **Intermediate and final report**：The next retained draft switched to product directions at two points in time and explicitly rejected an unsupported inheritance claim. The final report kept that wording.

Model-review findings are relayed through the author’s retained decisions.

## Coze’s models

Coze China groups native agents, third-party cloud agents, and local connections in one interface. An execution framework organizes actions; the underlying model handles understanding and reasoning.

**Coze supplies models for its cloud-hosted Claude Code.**

The report identifies Claude Code and Codex CLI as execution frameworks. A program’s brand does not, by itself, establish which model powers a particular deployment.

### Changes

**Initial draft · Translated excerpt**

> Coze brings native agents, third-party agents hosted in the cloud, and local connections into one collaboration interface. The native version includes platform scheduling, email, and cloud resources; the other modes do not automatically inherit those built-in services.

The section distinguished execution modes and services, but did not identify their specific model supply.

[Original passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/original.zh-CN.html#L63)

**Final report · Translated excerpt**

> Native agents can select models such as Doubao, Kimi, GLM, and MiniMax. Third-party cloud mode runs execution frameworks such as Claude Code and Codex CLI on Coze cloud computers, using models supplied by Coze rather than being tied to the original provider’s account and model.

This paragraph was added to the same product section. Its existing mode and permission analysis remains.

[Original passage (Chinese)](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/revised.zh-CN.html#L73)

**What changed**

- **Added**：Specific native-model choices and the distinction between third-party cloud frameworks and their model supply.
- **Retained**：The original service boundaries and local-device conditions; the additional paragraph does not make all three modes equivalent.

### Reasoning

**The fact layer missing from the draft**

General discussion of model importance and execution modes cannot answer which models a particular product uses. A reader may also mistake the Claude Code program brand for a model provider.

**What the additional sources establish**

The product introduction lists native-model choices. The cloud-agent document explains frameworks running on Coze computers with Coze-supplied models. They allow model supply and execution mode to be described separately.

**Decision and tradeoffs**

- **Chosen**：Add verifiable product configuration while retaining the distinct roles of models, tools, context, and permissions.
- **Not inferred**：Configuration documents do not measure a model’s share of time saved or real-task completion rates.
- **What is still needed**：Comparing model effects requires fixed tasks, inputs, tools, permissions, and result criteria, followed by errors, failed calls, and rework.

[Agent types](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/sources.html#S04) · [Product introduction](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/sources.html#S29) · [Third-party cloud documentation](https://rrrrrredy.github.io/research-toolkit/case-study/office-agents/sources.html#S30)

### Review

- **Initial draft**：The Coze section described execution modes and service boundaries. Its conclusion covered general model roles but lacked specific supply details.
- **Review finding**：Two model reviews found the analysis too general. The author combined their substantive findings and added product facts rather than duplicate paragraphs.
- **Expanded intermediate draft**：The author returned to official type, product, and cloud documents. The next draft added native choices and cloud-model supply, with product-level synthesis in the conclusion.
- **Later decision and final report**：A later adjudication added no duplicate mechanism paragraph. Conditions for controlled comparisons were retained; simply switching a model was not treated as a causal experiment.

Model-review findings are relayed through the author’s retained decisions.
