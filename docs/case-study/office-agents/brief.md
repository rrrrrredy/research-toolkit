# 国内办公 Agent 产品研究 / Office agent research in China

研究范围为 2026 年中国大陆用户能够接触的办公 Agent，包含独立工作台、桌面执行和办公套件中的能力。报告的信息截止日为 2026 年 9 月 9 日；部分动态文档于 9 月 10 日核对。依据公开资料，不包含产品安装实测。

The case examines office agents accessible to users in mainland China in 2026: independent workspaces, desktop execution, and capabilities within office suites. The information cutoff is September 9, 2026; some dynamic documents were checked on September 10. It uses public evidence and does not include installed-product testing.

## 研究问题 / Questions

- 产品处理什么工作对象？生成文件、修改已有文件和改变业务状态有什么区别？
- 发布、测试、购买与部署状态如何？地区、账号、套餐和组织授权带来什么限制？
- 模型、工具、上下文、权限和人工协助分别影响什么？
- 失败、返工、修改与协作交接如何改变实际收益？
- 有哪些采用或使用证据？哪些反例可能推翻主要判断？

These questions cover work objects and outcomes; release and access conditions; the roles of models, tools, context, permissions, and people; rework and handoff; and adoption evidence or counterexamples that could change the conclusions.

## 证据范围 / Evidence scope

优先官方帮助、更新、权限和计费文档，以及说明方法的使用记录。公司声明、媒体体验与实际效果数据分别处理；缺少总体数据时，不推算市场份额、稳定成功率或净人效。不将本研究当作安装指南、采购推荐或完整市场名录。

Prefer official product, release, permission, and pricing documentation, plus usage accounts that describe their methods. Keep company statements, media exercises, and measured outcomes distinct. Do not estimate market share, stable task success, or net productivity without adequate data. This report is not an installation guide, procurement recommendation, or comprehensive market directory.
