What changed when a Chinese GPAI report went through review?

On 19 November 2026, Codex reviewed a Chinese report about a general-purpose AI model provider entering the EU. The draft treated the EU representative requirement as generally applicable. After the auditor checked the conditions, the revised passage included the qualifying open-source exemption. Read the initial draft and final report in Chinese.
