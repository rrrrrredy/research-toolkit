Original passage (Chinese)

What changed

  • Shortened：Remove the rhetorical setup and state how materials, execution, and revisions share a workspace.
  • Made specific：Turn “implementations differ” into tools, context, and device availability. Keep the conditional benefit of less file handling.

Reasoning

Which wording adds little

Moving a chat box is not the mechanism examined in this paragraph. Using it as a contrast delays the point. Saying implementations differ gives the reader no criteria.

Why this revision helps

The final paragraph states the workspace mechanism and identifies three conditions. Readers can compare the products on tools, context, and device availability.

Decision and tradeoffs

  • Chosen：Express the judgment through actions, objects, and conditions, with less rhetorical setup.
  • Retained：Less file handling is a possible benefit; continuous operation and processing location still depend on the implementation.
  • Not inferred：This edit neither bans contrastive sentences nor identifies whether a passage was written by AI.

What changed

  • Narrowed：“Evidence of continuity” becomes product directions at two points in time, without a claim of version inheritance.
  • Retained：Both official product descriptions and the warning against carrying earlier capabilities over to the later product.

Reasoning

What each source supports

The April source describes aily operating on documents, multidimensional tables, and tasks. The late-August article describes Doubao Work using organizational context and returning results to collaboration.

Why the inference was narrowed

Similar functions and successive dates do not establish renaming, version inheritance, or feature migration. The author’s follow-up did not obtain that evidence, so wording implying continuity was removed.

Decision and tradeoffs

  • Chosen：Analyze each source without joining them into a verified product lineage.
  • Tradeoff：The product relationship stays unresolved. Comparisons must use capabilities supported by each source separately, without merging their feature lists.
  • What could change it：An explicit renaming notice, version mapping, or feature-migration document would support revisiting the relationship.

What changed

  • Added：Specific native-model choices and the distinction between third-party cloud frameworks and their model supply.
  • Retained：The original service boundaries and local-device conditions; the additional paragraph does not make all three modes equivalent.

Reasoning

The fact layer missing from the draft

General discussion of model importance and execution modes cannot answer which models a particular product uses. A reader may also mistake the Claude Code program brand for a model provider.

What the additional sources establish

The product introduction lists native-model choices. The cloud-agent document explains frameworks running on Coze computers with Coze-supplied models. They allow model supply and execution mode to be described separately.

Decision and tradeoffs

  • Chosen：Add verifiable product configuration while retaining the distinct roles of models, tools, context, and permissions.
  • Not inferred：Configuration documents do not measure a model’s share of time saved or real-task completion rates.
  • What is still needed：Comparing model effects requires fixed tasks, inputs, tools, permissions, and result criteria, followed by errors, failed calls, and rework.
