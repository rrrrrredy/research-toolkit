Research brief

Redo the original homepage question using primary English documentation available on 5 November 2026: when should an enterprise agent use a supported API, deterministic browser automation, model-directed visual control or a combination for recurring business work?

Write in English for operations and engineering leads. Compare actual operation coverage, completion evidence, retry semantics, authorization, uncertain outcomes, maintenance, migration and human handling. Explain a coherent hypothetical support workflow without presenting it as a deployed customer case. Give conditions for choosing and changing routes rather than a vendor ranking.

Preserve the original question and use of current documentation. Distinguish documented behavior, scenario assumptions and architectural judgment. No installed-product trial, fabricated benchmark, measured savings or comparative reliability claim is required or supported.

Develop research contributions progressively, then assemble a complete report. Seven independent content perspectives, a domain specialist, an independent audit and separate final acceptance examine the work. Author checks are preparation, not independent review. Keep the first full draft, source reading coverage, original review replies and revisions. The October case remains available as history.
