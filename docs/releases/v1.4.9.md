# Research Toolkit v1.4.9

[English](v1.4.9.md) | [简体中文](v1.4.9.zh-CN.md)

This patch distributes the three research-choice examples merged in [PR #110](https://github.com/rrrrrredy/research-toolkit/pull/110). The v1.4.8 plugin package does not contain them. Updating makes the examples available through the existing writing method, without changing public interfaces or saved-task formats.

## Changes

- **Develop an argument.** The predictive-maintenance example retains a useful downtime observation and explains the connections among attribution, economic value and multiyear prepayment. More explanation is needed, not just a caution about sample size.
- **Remove distractions.** The Scope-3 RFQ example replaces repeated writing advice with analysis of the buyer's purpose, factor suitability, coverage and assurance requirements. Necessary qualifications remain; shortest is not the objective.
- **Revise affected judgments.** The Maple storage example changes the recommendation after explicitly constructed budget and capacity requirements change its premises, retaining valid recovery-time comparisons and unknowns.

The first two are teaching adaptations of archived original and revised reports; the third adds a constructed follow-up. Their source material is synthetic. Original reports and historical records remain unchanged. The examples accept different supported wording and structures, and remain optional reading within the existing method. English, Chinese and plugin copies are synchronized; no tools, required fields, checks or review layers are added.

## What the comparisons observed

Two new constructed tasks used the same `gpt-6-astra` model, configuration, materials and other methods. Each method version generated one output per task, followed by one blinded review per pair: **2 tasks, 4 first outputs and 2 first reviews**. The original prose, requests, method snapshots, anonymous review inputs/outputs and timings remain in the [comparison archive](https://github.com/rrrrrredy/research-toolkit/blob/v1.4.9/evals/studies/2026-10-27-research-writing-choices/README.md).

| Task | Local addition with the examples | Omissions and unchanged findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archive-digitization procurement | Derived a license price near RMB 10,700 for cost parity and made the starting labor plan more explicit. | A separate 400-hour savings threshold was not explained as unreachable within the current printed-forms-only scope, whose entire manual workload is about 213.3 hours. Trial labor was not explicitly constrained by the budget. Both versions already recovered omitted labor and compared complete costs correctly. |
| Cold-chain revision | Explained why one hour of thermal-test headroom is not a guaranteed rescue window: waiting and transfer consume it. | Route-specific backup arrival terms were less explicit than before. Both drafts still need to distinguish availability this season from delivery next week; both already made the correct selection after the new orders and price. |

**Local improvements accompanied omissions. No consistent whole-report improvement or reduced substantive rework was observed.** Human rework time was not measured, and these pairs do not isolate the contribution of each example. The first outputs were not repaired and then counted as generation results.

| Task | Before | With examples | First blinded review |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Archive digitization | 84.938 s | 84.109 s | 170.594 s |
| Cold-chain revision | 59.781 s | 62.907 s | 137.672 s |

Author processes total 291.735 s; reviews total 308.266 s; all six CLI model calls total 600.001 s. These are process wall times including startup and service latency, excluding preparation and maintainer reading. Each author input increased by 1,893 tokens. No speedup or general quality gain is inferred.
