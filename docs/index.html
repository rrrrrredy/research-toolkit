Initial draftTranslated excerpt

The value of independent office agents lies not in moving a chat box onto the desktop, but in bringing materials, execution, and revisions into one stretch of work. For people who frequently organize files from different sources, this approach can reduce uploading, downloading, and switching tools. Yet keeping files on a computer does not mean tasks run continuously or all processing happens offline. The differences between Kimi Work and Coze show that independent workspaces do not share a single implementation.

The opening frames value through a rhetorical contrast; the closing only says implementations differ.

Final reportTranslated excerpt

Independent office agents organize materials, execution, and revisions in one workspace. For people who frequently organize files from different sources, this approach can reduce uploading, downloading, and switching tools. Continuous operation and the location of data processing still depend on the implementation. The differences between Kimi Work and Coze center on who supplies tools, who retains context, and who keeps the device online.

The report states the mechanism directly and identifies comparison criteria, while retaining its conditions.

Usage

Give your agent a research question and ask it to read SKILL.md, the shared entry point for the research methods.

If repository access is unavailable, upload the files or paste the instructions. Native Skill installation is optional.

Reading or uploading the methods does not start MCP or run an independent review. The tools need local stdio MCP support; the plugin also requires its package format.

Full uses a degraded self-review when no reviewer backend is configured. Choose an external command or compatible endpoint for another opinion; independent review requires a separate context. External calls send the supplied materials to that service and consume its account usage. Lite retains a final checklist and skips review.
