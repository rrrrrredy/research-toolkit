APIs or browsers for AI agents

The decision belongs to the individual operation. An agent may read a ticket through an API, generate a document through an administrative screen and attach the result through another API. Calling the whole system an “API agent” or a “browser agent” tells an operations lead little about whether a failed run can be resumed without repeating work.

October reportOriginal English excerpt

Resume from the unresolved operation and inspect whether its result exists before creating another one. If the administrative interface cannot reveal enough state to settle that question, route the case to a person.

The October report gave the general recovery principle.

November reportOriginal English excerpt

On resumption, inspect history for the same reference. A matching completed job supplies the document ID. A pending job should be followed to its outcome. Another submission is justified only by authoritative evidence that the earlier attempt did not start, or by a documented mechanism that makes repetition safe.

The November study develops that principle within a hypothetical support workflow; this is not a deployed test.
