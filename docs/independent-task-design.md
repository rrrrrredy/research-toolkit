# Independent tasks and a future held-out study

[English](independent-task-design.md) · [简体中文](independent-task-design.zh-CN.md)

The current five-family pack and four-family judge controls are public development inputs. They stay exposed even after a method changes. The [intake contract](../evals/templates/heldout-intake.json) defines the next study's handoff; it contains **no admitted tasks**, and does not claim a completed external dataset. A formal efficacy study is a separate execution decision after the bounded development cycle.

## Roles and access

1. Freeze the method commit and instruction hashes before independent task authors receive their assignment. Give them the target workload, language mix, source-rights requirements and time boundaries; do not give them observed Toolkit wins/losses, candidate reports, tuning instructions or development answer keys. A fresh model context is a declared authorship boundary, not proof of intellectual or training independence.
2. A named custodian keeps full briefs, sources, expected necessary judgments and acceptable alternatives separate from method development. Record authorship, dates, access events and every rejected candidate with its pre-outcome reason. The implementation author must not relabel self-authored or previously inspected tuning cases as external holdout.
3. Admit cases against source sufficiency, realistic user constraints, unambiguous necessary judgments and publishable provenance before any author output exists. Include valid alternatives and explicit uncertainty; a reference answer is not the only acceptable prose. A domain overlap is not automatically disqualifying, but recycled facts, translations and paraphrases keep the original family identity.
4. Seal the admitted packet hashes and cohort order. If a developer sees a packet before the method freeze, retain that exposure and move it to development. If an error is found after generation starts, keep the task and original results visible as defective-input evidence; do not silently replace it or count its replacement as the same held-out test.

## Design to freeze before calls

Specify the intended claim first: defect detection, reader usefulness, live-source research, or a causal effect of the Toolkit. Match baseline and Toolkit author model, sources/retrieval access, output protocol, requested effort and limits. Report the extra Toolkit instruction tokens as a resource difference. Fixed-source instruction-only Lite does not test retrieval, persistent source ledgers, Full review or long-running workflows.

Record a sample-size rationale based on the desired precision and family-level discordance, not an attractive model–task count. Use development results to plan, then freeze the new sample size before held-out outcomes. Languages, author models, repeats and swaps are strata or sensitivity checks, not extra independent families. Keep all admitted execution failures in the denominator; report complete-pair results alongside missingness rather than assigning missing reports a quality score.

For claims under the current [report evaluation standard](report-evaluation-standard.md), predeclare its four-reviewer panel and any required calibration. Exclude each report's author model from its independent judge panel. If four API author models and Astra are available, the three other API models plus Astra can be a proposed panel for each author stratum, subject to separate calibration, budget and availability checks. These shared models remain correlated judges; agreement is not ground truth. An Astra-only developmental diagnosis is not that formal panel.

Use the same brief, sources, rubric and verbatim A/B reports in fresh judge contexts. Conceal author names, conditions, notes, manifests, expected judgments and execution metadata. Freeze randomization, primary orientation, swaps, label decoding, failure treatment and source-based adjudication before results. Disclose residual condition clues; do not clean them out of reports afterward.

Write an exact request/token/time plan covering authors, all judge orientations and calibration separately. Fix the stopping rule: exhaust the admitted bounded cohort unless a documented operational stop occurs; preserve partial runs; no automatic paid retries, winner-dependent expansion or favorable-orientation selection. Formal quality claims require adjudication against the sources and uncertainty reporting at the family level.

## Acceptance and current boundary

The design deliverable is complete when these roles, records, freeze conditions and acceptance rules are reviewable. Dataset collection is complete only after real independent authorship and rights/access records exist. Execution is complete only after all admitted first attempts, including failures, are retained and analyzed. The empty contract is intentionally not accepted by the exposed-development schema-2 runner. No current documentation should count it as additional tasks or held-out efficacy evidence.
