# From individual review perspectives to versioned assignments

[Design decisions](README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/rfcs/0001-versioned-review.md)

Earlier full-report plans assigned independent contexts to individual perspectives. Hosts vary in how many sessions they can run, and several related checks benefit from reading the same material together. A new v3 plan assigns seven perspectives to three independent groups, adds specialists where needed and reserves separate audit and final checks. Hosts dispatch work within their available slots; two slots can serve successive assignments.

Each response binds its manuscript and assignment versions. Reinterpreting a saved plan changes the historical record. Continuing a task uses its saved plan and checks any changed manuscript against its bound inputs.
